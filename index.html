Your audiobooks, on your shelves.

AudioAlcove turns the audiobooks in your Plex or Audiobookshelf library, or the files on your iPhone, into a collection you can hold.

Coming soon to the App Store

Shelves to browse

Books stand on wooden shelves by their spines, or face out as covers in the Classic look. Series, authors and genres each have a shelf.

Plex, Audiobookshelf and Files

Sign in on plex.tv, connect an Audiobookshelf server, or add audiobook files from your iPhone. Your progress goes back to your server.

Read along

Attach a DRM-free EPUB or PDF and follow the words as they are read, or make a transcript on your iPhone.

Listening tools

Trim Silence, Voice Boost, Smart Rewind, chapters, bookmarks with notes and a sleep timer.

Downloads

Keep books on your iPhone for flights and drives, and fetch the next book in a series before you need it.

Stats and badges

Streaks, a daily goal, a Year in Review and 70 engraved badges to earn.
