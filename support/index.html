Something not working, or an idea for AudioAlcove? Open an issue on GitHub and describe what happened, with your iPhone model and iOS version. Please never include a password or token.

Ask a question

Common questions

What do I need?

Any one of these: a Plex Media Server with an audiobook library, an Audiobookshelf server, or audiobook files saved on your iPhone or in iCloud Drive. AudioAlcove runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

Can I try it without a server?

Yes. On the welcome screen, tap Try Two Sample Books. AudioAlcove adds two public-domain LibriVox recordings, The Happy Prince and Other Tales by Oscar Wilde and The Gift of the Magi by O. Henry, to the On This iPhone shelf.

Why does AudioAlcove ask for local network access?

Most Plex and Audiobookshelf servers live on a home network. Local network access lets AudioAlcove reach yours. Without it, only servers reachable over the internet will work.

My Plex server does not appear

Make sure the server is signed in to the same Plex account, or shared with it, and that it is running. Away from home, the server needs Plex's Remote Access turned on. If your Plex account has several Home profiles, use Switch Profile in Settings to pick the right one.

How do I add audiobooks from my iPhone?

Choose Add from Files, then pick audiobook files or whole folders. AudioAlcove reads m4b, m4a, mp3, aac and flac files. A folder becomes one book, and files that share an album and author are joined into one book.

How does read-along work?

On a book's page, attach a DRM-free EPUB or PDF of the same book, and the words follow the narration as you listen. Without an ebook, you can make a transcript on your iPhone instead. It is made entirely on the device.

Does my progress sync?

Your place in each book is saved to your Plex or Audiobookshelf server, so other apps see it too. Stats, bookmarks, shelves and badges sync to your other devices through your own iCloud account.

How do I sign out?

Open Settings in AudioAlcove and choose Sign Out under Account. Signing out of Plex removes the books downloaded from it. Your stats, bookmarks, shelves and the books you added from Files stay.
