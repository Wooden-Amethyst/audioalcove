Privacy Support

Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

AudioAlcove collects no data. There is no AudioAlcove account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Rhys Ellwood, who makes AudioAlcove, never receives your library, your listening history or anything else about you.

What stays on your iPhone

Your library list, downloaded books, ebooks you attach, transcripts, bookmarks, listening stats, badges and settings are kept on your iPhone. Deleting AudioAlcove deletes them.

Services AudioAlcove talks to for you

Plex

You sign in on plex.tv in Apple's secure sign-in sheet, so AudioAlcove never sees your Plex password. Plex gives the app an access token, which is kept in the iOS Keychain. AudioAlcove uses it to ask plex.tv for your servers, then talks to your Plex Media Server directly to list and play your audiobooks and to save your listening progress there. Plex's own privacy policy covers plex.tv.

Audiobookshelf

If you connect an Audiobookshelf server, its address and your sign-in token are kept in the iOS Keychain. AudioAlcove talks only to that server.

iCloud

When you are signed in to iCloud, your listening sessions, finished books, bookmarks, shelves, badges and settings are kept in your own private iCloud database, so they follow you to your other devices. Apple stores this data for you, and the developer cannot see it. You can turn it off in the Settings app under your name, then iCloud.

Finding missing covers

When a book has no cover, AudioAlcove sends its title and author to Open Library, run by the Internet Archive, to look for one. Nothing else is sent. You can turn this off with Find Missing Artwork in AudioAlcove's Settings.

Transcripts

Transcripts are made on your iPhone with Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio never leaves your iPhone. The first time, iOS may download a language model from Apple.

Sample books

The two sample books come with the app, so trying them sends nothing anywhere.

Permissions

AudioAlcove asks to use your local network so it can reach a Plex or Audiobookshelf server in your home. It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

AudioAlcove collects no information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Delete AudioAlcove to remove everything it keeps on your iPhone. In AudioAlcove's Settings, Transcript Storage can also delete every transcript on its own. To remove the iCloud copy, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then manage your account storage, and delete AudioAlcove's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
